About Aqualeo

A senior team built for long-term partnerships.

We're a focused IT consulting and digital commerce firm operating across London, Dubai and Bangalore — combining global delivery with local accountability.

Aqualeo Digecom was founded by senior practitioners who'd spent decades inside large consultancies and product organisations — and were tired of the trade-offs. We built the firm we wished existed: small enough to care, senior enough to deliver, structured enough to scale.

Today we partner with mid-market and enterprise teams across the UK, GCC and India. We embed alongside your people, transfer knowledge as we go, and measure success in business outcomes — not just deliverables.
